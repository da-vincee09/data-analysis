# AI & the Job Market: Adoption vs. Risk

## Overview

This project is an interactive Tableau dashboard and story exploring how AI adoption is reshaping the modern job market. Using a synthetic-but-realistic dataset from Kaggle, the analysis examines the relationship between AI adoption levels, automation risk, salary, required skills, and job growth projections across industries.

The dashboards allow viewers to explore which industries pay the most, whether higher AI adoption correlates with higher automation risk, which skills are most in-demand versus which ones pay best, and where remote-friendly work and company size shape compensation.

> **Note:** This project was independently designed and developed using a synthetic AI job market dataset from Kaggle as part of my learning journey in Tableau data visualization and dashboard development.

**View the live dashboard:** [AI & the Job Market: Adoption vs. Risk (Tableau Public)](https://public.tableau.com/views/AI_Job_Market_Adoption_vs_Risk/AItheJobMarketAdoptionvs_Risk?:language=en-US&publish=yes)

## Objectives

- Clean and prepare the AI job market dataset
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) across industries, skills, and salary
- Build calculated fields to support risk scoring and percentage-based comparisons
- Analyze the relationship between AI adoption and automation risk
- Design a multi-dashboard Tableau workbook using varied, non-default chart types
- Build a guided Tableau Story to present findings with a clear narrative and takeaway

## Dataset

**AI-Powered Job Market Insights**

Source: [Kaggle — AI-Powered Job Market Insights](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uom190346a/ai-powered-job-market-insights)

**This dataset is synthetic** — it was generated to realistically simulate job market patterns rather than sourced from real job postings, and is used here for practice and portfolio purposes.

The dataset contains job listings with:

- Job Title
- Industry
- Company Size
- Location
- AI Adoption Level
- Automation Risk
- Required Skills
- Salary (USD)
- Remote-Friendly Status
- Job Growth Projection
